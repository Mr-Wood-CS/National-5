You are going to make a program for a teacher.

It will store three pupil names and scores, then work out the total and average.

## What the Program Should Do

Ask for:

- the name of pupil 1
- the score for pupil 1
- the name of pupil 2
- the score for pupil 2
- the name of pupil 3
- the score for pupil 3

Then display:

- each pupil's name and score
- the total score
- the average score
